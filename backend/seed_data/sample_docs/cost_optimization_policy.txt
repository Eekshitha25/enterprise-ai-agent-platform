Internal Cloud Cost Optimization Policy (v2)

1. All SageMaker training jobs must have a maximum runtime limit configured;
   jobs without an explicit timeout will be flagged by the FinOps agent.
2. Cross-region data transfer for training datasets should be avoided by
   replicating source data into the training region ahead of time.
3. Any month-over-month service cost increase greater than 25% triggers an
   automatic anomaly report to the engineering lead responsible for that service.
4. Reserved Instances / Savings Plans should be reviewed quarterly against
   actual EC2 usage patterns.
