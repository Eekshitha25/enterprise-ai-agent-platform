Engineering Onboarding FAQ

Q: How do I request AWS access?
A: Submit a request through the internal IT portal; access is provisioned
   via IAM roles scoped to your team's project, not individual users.

Q: Where can I find architecture diagrams?
A: They're stored in Confluence under Engineering > Architecture, and mirrored
   in the docs/ARCHITECTURE.md file in each service's repo.

Q: Who do I contact about a cost overrun?
A: File a ticket with the FinOps team; they review the Cost Optimization Policy
   and can pause non-critical jobs if needed.
